FreeSolo turns the city you're in into a game. Each **level** is a short
history lesson tied to a real place: go there, look closely, answer the
challenge, and the story behind it unlocks.

## Start a journey

You can play **Tangier, Porto, Córdoba, Rotterdam and Salzburg**, with more
cities on the way.

<Steps>
<Step>
**Open [Play](/play)** and pick your city.
</Step>
<Step>
**Set your days.** On the city page, say how many days you have (and, if you
like, when you arrive). FreeSolo orders the levels into walks and splits them
across your days, shown as a path of levels next to the map.
</Step>
<Step>
**Tap the next level and head there.** *Walk me there* opens directions.
</Step>
</Steps>

Free levels need no account. Your progress is kept in your browser until you
sign in; once signed in, it's saved to your account.

## Solving a level

Each level opens with a photo of the place, a one-line **hook** and a
**challenge** you can only answer by being there. The photo stays blurred until
you solve it, so it can't give the answer away.

Solve it and the photo comes into focus. You get the key facts at a glance, a
short story (tap **Read the whole story** for more), **Listen** to hear it read
aloud, **Watch videos** about the place, and its sources.

- **Type what you found.** Spelling mistakes are fine, and so are other
  languages.
- **Or add a photo**, where photo checks are switched on.
- **Stuck?** Take a hint. Each one lowers the stardust for that level a little.

<Callout type="info">
  Answers are matched against what the level expects. Where AI checking is
  switched on, it also understands your own wording and photos, and gives you a
  nudge when you're close — without giving the answer away.
</Callout>

## Points and star ranks

Every mission shows its points: free starter missions are worth ✨ 100, and
harder or further-flung ones up to ✨ 250. Solve it and you earn them as
**stardust**. Each hint costs a fifth of the mission's points (never more than
half). Stardust from every city adds up, and your rank evolves as it grows:

| Rank | Stardust |
| --- | --- |
| Nebula | 0 |
| Protostar | 100 |
| White Dwarf | 400 |
| Red Giant | 1,000 |
| Blue Giant | 2,000 |
| Supernova | 4,000 |
| Black Hole | 8,000 |

Ranks are earned, never bought. Replaying a level you've solved doesn't earn
more stardust.

## Global ranking

[Ranking](/ranking) lists the players with the most stardust — **all time**
and **this week** (the last seven days) — with a podium for the top three.
Names are shortened, like "Hana O.". Sign in to appear: progress kept only in
your browser doesn't count.

## Your sky

[My sky](/sky) shows a star for every level you've solved, placed where it
stands in the city — so each city's constellation has the shape of your walk.
Finish every level in a city and its constellation joins up. Below it, your
**postcards** collect a photo of every place you've solved.

Photos come from Wikimedia Commons; see [Photo credits](/docs/photo-credits).

## City packs

The first levels in every city are free. A **city pack** unlocks the rest of
that city for good, to replay whenever you like. While FreeSolo is in pilot,
packs are free: sign in and tap **Unlock**.

## Where do the facts come from?

Lessons are written from published sources, and every story links to them. If
you think something's wrong, [open an issue](https://github.com/usmhic/freesolo/issues) and
we'll check it — lessons live in the open-source repository, so fixes are public too.
