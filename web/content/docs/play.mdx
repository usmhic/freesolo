FreeSolo turns the city you're in into a game. Each **level** is a short
history lesson tied to a real place: go there, look closely, answer the
challenge, and the story behind it unlocks.

## Start a journey

<Steps>
<Step>
**Open [Play](/play)** and pick your city.
</Step>
<Step>
**Say when you arrive and how many days you have.** FreeSolo orders the
levels into a walk and splits them across your days.
</Step>
<Step>
**Tap a level and head there.** *Walk me there* opens directions.
</Step>
</Steps>

Free levels need no account. Your progress is kept in your browser until you
sign in; once signed in, it's saved to your account.

## Solving a level

Each level has a **hook** (why this place matters), a **challenge** (something
to find or work out on the spot) and, once you solve it, **the story** with
links to its sources.

- **Type what you found.** Spelling mistakes are fine, and so are other
  languages.
- **Or add a photo**, where photo checks are switched on.
- **Stuck?** Take a hint. Each one lowers the stardust for that level a little.

<Callout type="info">
  Answers are matched against what the level expects. Where AI checking is
  switched on, it also understands your own wording and photos, and gives you a
  nudge when you're close — without giving the answer away.
</Callout>

## Stardust and star ranks

A solved level earns **stardust**: 150 without hints, a little less with them.
Stardust from every city adds up, and your rank evolves as it grows:

| Rank | Stardust |
| --- | --- |
| Nebula | 0 |
| Protostar | 100 |
| White Dwarf | 400 |
| Red Giant | 1,000 |
| Blue Giant | 2,000 |
| Supernova | 4,000 |
| Black Hole | 8,000 |

Ranks are earned, never bought. Replaying a level you've solved doesn't earn
more stardust.

## Your sky

[My sky](/sky) shows a star for every level you've solved, placed where it
stands in the city — so each city's constellation has the shape of your walk.
Finish every level in a city and its constellation joins up.

## City packs

The first levels in every city are free. A **city pack** unlocks the rest of
that city for good, to replay whenever you like. While FreeSolo is in pilot,
packs are free: sign in and tap **Unlock**.

## Where do the facts come from?

Lessons are written from published sources, and every story links to them. If
you think something's wrong, [open an issue](https://github.com/usmhic/freesolo/issues) and
we'll check it — lessons live in the open-source repository, so fixes are public too.
