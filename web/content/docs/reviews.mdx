Reviews are how FreeSolo stays trustworthy without feeling corporate — real
notes from real travelers, visible right on each experience and host's profile.

## Leaving a review

Once an experience you've booked is marked **completed**, you'll get a gentle
nudge — *"How was [experience name]?"* — in your notifications. From there (or
from the booking itself in your **Profile → Past trips**):

1. Choose a rating from **1 to 5 stars**.
2. Write at least a short note (10 characters minimum — though a sentence or
   two that captures what stood out goes a lot further for the next traveler
   deciding whether to book).

A few things worth knowing:

- You can only review experiences you actually attended — specifically, a
  booking that reached **confirmed** or **completed** status.
- You can leave **one review per booking** — if you book the same host's
  experience again later, you're welcome to share a fresh take then too.

<Callout type="info">
  Your review is tied to the host (and the experience), and shows up
  publicly — so other travelers browsing that host's profile or experience page
  can see it before they book.
</Callout>

## Replying as a host

If you host experiences, every review left on your listings is a chance to
say thanks, add context, or simply close the loop publicly. Open the review
from your dashboard and add a reply — it appears right alongside the original
review, visible to anyone reading it later.

Only the host of the reviewed experience can reply to that review — so
travelers always know they're hearing from the person who was actually there.

## Why reviews matter on FreeSolo

Because every experience is small and every host is a real community member
(not an agency), reviews carry a lot of weight here. A handful of honest,
specific reviews — *"Rui let us try the wheel ourselves and didn't rush
anyone"* — does more for trust than any star rating alone. When you're deciding
what to book next, they're usually the fastest way to know whether an
experience lives up to its description; see [Finding an
experience](/docs/exploring#tips-for-finding-the-right-fit) for more on reading
them well.
