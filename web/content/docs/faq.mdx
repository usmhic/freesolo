## 🚀 Getting started

<Accordions>

<Accordion title="Do I need an invite code to apply?">

No — applying is open to anyone. The invite code is something *you* receive
**after** your application is approved, and you'll use it (along with your
email and a password you set) to sign in for the first time. See [Joining
FreeSolo](/docs/joining) for the full walkthrough.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How long does the application review take?">

Around 48 hours in most cases. You'll get an email either way — approved or
not — so there's no need to keep checking back.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do I check where my application got to?">

Use the **reference** from your confirmation email at
[Check application status](/apply/status). It'll show *in review*, *approved*,
or *not this time* — no sign-in needed.

Lost the reference? It's only ever sent to you, so we can't look it up from an
email address alone. Sit tight for the decision email, which arrives either way.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="I applied but haven't heard back. What should I do?">

Double check the email address you applied with for our confirmation message
(and your spam folder). Applications are reviewed in the order they arrive, so
during busier periods it may take a little longer than usual.

</Accordion>

</Accordions>

## 🎟️ Booking

<Accordions>

<Accordion title="Does FreeSolo take my payment?">

No. FreeSolo reserves your seat; it never charges you. Whatever the venue
charges for the experience, you settle with them directly on the day.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Why is my booking still “pending”?">

Because the experience hasn't reached its minimum group size yet. Bookings only
flip to **confirmed** once enough travelers have joined — see [Booking a
seat](/docs/booking#why-pending-comes-before-confirmed) for the full
explanation.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What happens if an experience never fills up?">

Your reservation simply lapses. No action needed on your end.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I cancel after the group has confirmed?">

Yes. Open the booking from your profile, tap **Cancel**, and add a quick
reason. Do it as early as you can so the host isn't left short on the day.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How many seats can I book at once?">

Between 1 and 4 — and only as many as the experience has remaining. The seat
selector will automatically grey out options that would overbook the group.

</Accordion>

</Accordions>

## 🏠 Hosting

<Accordions>

<Accordion title="Who can host an experience?">

Any **approved** FreeSolo member with an **approved** venue. If you haven't
applied yet, start with [Joining FreeSolo](/docs/joining); once you're a
verified member, [Hosting an experience](/docs/hosting) walks through
registering your venue and publishing your first listing.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How long does venue approval take?">

Usually around three business days. Your **Business dashboard** will clearly
show an *"in review"* state the whole time, so you'll always know where things
stand.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do travelers pay me?">

Directly, at your venue, however you normally take payment. FreeSolo doesn't
process money — it brings you a confirmed group and tells you who's coming.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What's the difference between “pending” and “confirmed” on my dashboard?">

**Pending** means the group needs a few more travelers before it locks in —
your dashboard will even tell you exactly how many (*"needs 2 more to
confirm"*). **Confirmed** means the minimum has been met and the experience is
happening.

</Accordion>

</Accordions>

## ⚙️ Account & technical

<Accordions>

<Accordion title="How do I change my profile photo?">

From your **Profile**, tap directly on your avatar and choose a new photo from
your library. It updates everywhere instantly.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="I'm not seeing real-time updates — what gives?">

Pull down to refresh on the Feed, Profile, Notifications, or Business
dashboard at any time to fetch the latest. Most screens also refresh
automatically when you navigate back to them.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Something looks broken — who do I tell?">

If a screen shows a "Couldn't load…" message, tap **Try again** first — it's
often a brief connectivity hiccup. If it persists, reach out via the contact
details in your application confirmation email and the team will take a look.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Where's the technical API documentation?">

If you're a developer working on the FreeSolo platform itself (rather than
using the app), the full REST API reference now lives behind the **Admin
dashboard → API Docs** as an interactive Swagger / OpenAPI explorer — separate
from this guide, which is written for travelers and hosts.

</Accordion>

</Accordions>
