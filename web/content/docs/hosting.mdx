title: Hosting an experience
description: Registering your venue and publishing your first experience
---

Know your city better than the guidebooks do? FreeSolo lets approved members
flip from traveler to host — register a venue, publish an experience, and
start welcoming small groups of travelers.

<Callout type="warn">
  Hosting requires an **approved** FreeSolo account — see [Joining
  FreeSolo](/docs/joining) if you haven't applied yet. If you try to register a
  venue while signed out, the app will simply prompt you to sign in first.
</Callout>

## Step 1 — Register your venue

From your **Profile**, tap **Host experience**, or open **Business dashboard →
Register your venue**. The form is two short steps:

<Steps>
<Step>
**Venue info.** Name, type (studio, restaurant, bar, gallery, outdoor, café…),
city, and street address. These four are required — everything else is optional
polish.
</Step>
<Step>
**Online presence.** A Google Maps link, TripAdvisor page, Instagram handle,
and a short description of what makes your venue special. None of these are
required, but at least one goes a long way toward helping travelers trust your
listing before they book.
</Step>
</Steps>

Tap **Submit for review**. You'll see a confirmation that FreeSolo reviews
every venue before it goes live — usually within about three business days.
While it's in review, your **Business dashboard** will show an *"in review"*
state so you always know where things stand.

## Step 2 — Get approved, then connect payouts

Once your venue is approved, open your **Business dashboard** and connect your
payout details through **Stripe Connect** — a short identity-verification and
bank-details flow. This is what allows FreeSolo to send your venue's share of
each booking (and your travel credits) directly to you. See [Payments](/docs/payments)
for how the revenue split works.

## Step 3 — Publish your first experience

From **Host experience** (or the **+** action on your dashboard), fill out:

- **An icon and title** — pick from a curated set of emoji, or write something
  evocative like *"Sunrise Sintra Hike"*.
- **A description** — what travelers will actually do, and what makes it feel
  authentic rather than generic.
- **A category** — Art & Culture, Food & Drink, Outdoor, Music, Photography,
  History, Wellness, or Learning. This is how travelers will find you in the
  feed's filters.
- **Your venue** — pulled automatically from your approved business listings.
- **Date, time, and duration.**
- **Minimum and maximum group size** — the floor and ceiling for how many
  people make the experience work. A pottery class might cap at 6; a walking
  tour might comfortably hold 8.
- **Price per seat.**

Once published, your experience appears in the public feed immediately —
status **active** — and starts collecting reservations.

## Running your venue: the dashboard

Your **Business dashboard** is mission control:

- **Revenue this month**, **live experiences**, and **unique travelers** —
  three at-a-glance stats pulled from your real bookings.
- **Upcoming experiences**, each showing how full it is and whether it's
  trending toward **confirmed** (met its minimum) or still **pending**
  (needs a few more travelers) — with a friendly nudge like *"needs 2 more to
  confirm"*.
- **The revenue split reminder** — the same 70/10/20 breakdown your guests see,
  so there's never any ambiguity about what you'll receive.

Pull down to refresh at any time to see new bookings as they land.

## What happens when a group fills up

The moment your experience hits its minimum headcount, FreeSolo automatically:

1. Confirms every pending booking for that experience — no manual step on your
   end.
2. Notifies and emails each traveler that they're locked in.
3. Marks your dashboard card as **✓ Confirmed**.

From there, it's just about showing up and doing what you do best.
