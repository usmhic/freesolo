---
title: Joining FreeSolo
description: Why FreeSolo is invite-gated, how to apply, and what happens next
---

FreeSolo isn't an open marketplace — it's a community. To keep group
experiences feeling safe and genuinely social (instead of like a tour bus full
of strangers), every new member is reviewed before they can book or host.

## How to apply

From the **"I am a…"** welcome screen, choose **Solo Traveler → Get started**.
You'll land on a short application with three things to share:

<Steps>
<Step>
**Tell us your story.** A short write-up about who you are and how you travel
— at least 80 characters, so give it a real paragraph. This is the main thing
reviewers read, so let your personality show through.
</Step>
<Step>
**Leave your contact details.** An email address and phone number so we can
reach you about your application and, later, about your bookings.
</Step>
<Step>
**Add a photo (optional, but encouraged).** A friendly face makes your
application feel real — and it'll become your profile picture if you're approved.
</Step>
</Steps>

Tap **Submit application** and you're done — no account or password needed yet.
You'll immediately get a confirmation email, and the app will let you know
we'll review things within about 48 hours.

<Callout type="info">
  Applying twice with the same email won't create a duplicate — we'll just let
  you know we already have your application on file.
</Callout>

## While you wait

Your application sits in a review queue where a real person reads it (see
[how reviewers see it](#what-happens-on-our-side) below, if you're curious).
There's nothing to do on your end — just keep an eye on your inbox.

## Getting approved

If you're approved, you'll receive an email with an invite code in the format
`FRSLO-XXXXXX`. From there:

1. Open the app and choose **Sign in** (or **Create account**, depending on
   whether one already exists for your email).
2. Sign in with the email and password you set.
3. Your profile will show a **✓ Verified member** badge — and from that moment,
   you can reserve seats on any experience and, if you'd like, register a venue
   or host your own.

## If you're not approved this time

We'll let you know by email either way — FreeSolo is small by design, and
sometimes it's simply a matter of timing or fit rather than anything you did
wrong. You're welcome to apply again down the line.

## What happens on our side

For transparency: applications are reviewed by the FreeSolo team through an
internal dashboard. A reviewer reads your story, marks the application
**approved** or **rejected**, and can leave an internal note. Approving an
application is what flips your account from *pending* to *approved* and
triggers your invite-code email — there's no separate "activation" step beyond
that.
