Once you're signed in, the **Explore** tab is home base — a feed of small-group
experiences happening near you (or in whichever city you're planning to visit).

## The feed

Each card in the feed gives you the essentials at a glance:

- **What it is** — an emoji and title (*"Old City Pottery Morning"*, *"Jazz
  Bars of Alfama"*…) plus a short description of what you'll actually do.
- **When and where** — the date, time, and neighbourhood.
- **Who's hosting** — the local running it, and the venue it's based at.
- **How full it is** — a small progress bar showing seats filled vs. the
  maximum, so you can see at a glance whether a group is close to confirming or
  just getting started.
- **The price per seat.**

### Filtering by category

A row of chips along the top — **Art, Food, Music, Photo, Outdoor, History**,
and more — lets you narrow the feed to whatever mood you're in. Tap one to
filter, tap **All** to reset. Filters and the feed both respect whichever city
you're currently exploring.

### Pull to refresh

Pull down on the feed at any time to fetch the latest experiences — handy if
you're checking back to see whether a group you're eyeing has filled up.

### When something goes wrong

If the feed can't load (no connection, a server hiccup), you'll see a clear
message instead of a blank screen, with a **Try again** button — no mystery
spinners, no silent failures.

## Opening an experience

Tap any card to see the full picture:

- The complete description — what to expect, what's included, what to bring.
- The host's profile, so you know a bit about who you'd be spending the
  afternoon with.
- The venue it's hosted at (with its own profile — address, links to its
  socials and listings).
- A detailed seat tracker: how many people have joined, how many spots remain,
  and how close the group is to its **minimum** (the headcount needed for the
  experience to actually run — more on that on the [booking](/docs/booking) page).
- Reviews from people who've already been.

From here, **Reserve a seat** takes you into the booking flow.

## Featured experiences

A small, rotating selection of standout experiences is highlighted near the top
of the app — newly published, well-reviewed, or simply ones the FreeSolo team
thinks are worth a look. They behave exactly like any other experience; the
"featured" tag is just a spotlight.

## Tips for finding the right fit

- **Check the seats-filled bar.** An experience that's almost at its minimum is
  more likely to lock in soon — great if you want certainty. One that's just
  getting started has more flexibility on your end (you can usually still
  cancel before it confirms), but might take longer to happen.
- **Read the host's bio.** Hosts are real community members, and their profile
  often hints at the kind of experience you're signing up for — laid-back,
  detail-oriented, chatty, etc.
- **Look at past reviews.** They're the fastest way to know whether an
  experience lives up to its description.
