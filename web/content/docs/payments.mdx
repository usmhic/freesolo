title: Payments & the price breakdown
description: What you're charged, where it goes, and how refunds work
---

FreeSolo handles payments through [Stripe](https://stripe.com) — the same
infrastructure trusted by major travel and marketplace apps — so your card
details never touch FreeSolo's servers directly.

## What you actually pay

The price you see on an experience (say, **€28 per seat**) is multiplied by the
number of seats you reserve. That's your total — there are no hidden booking
fees or surprise charges added at checkout.

## Where your money goes

Every booking is split three ways the moment it's confirmed. FreeSolo shows you
this breakdown right on the booking screen, because we think it's worth being
upfront about:

| Share | Goes to | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **70% — Venue** | The local business hosting the experience | Covers the space, materials, food, and everything that makes the experience possible |
| **10% — Host credit** | The community member who organized it | Paid out as **travel credits** — see [Travel credits](/docs/credits) |
| **20% — Platform** | FreeSolo | Keeps the lights on — community review, support, building the app |

So on a €28 pottery morning for two seats (€56 total), roughly €39 goes
straight to the studio, €5.60 becomes travel credit for your host, and the
remaining ~€11 supports FreeSolo itself.

## When you're actually charged

This is the part that's different from most booking apps — and worth
repeating from the [booking guide](/docs/booking):

**You authorize a payment when you reserve, but you're only actually charged
once the experience's group reaches its minimum headcount.** If the group
never fills within the window, nothing is charged and your hold simply expires.

## Saving a card

The first time you book, you'll be prompted to securely save a card via
Stripe — so future bookings are a single tap. Your card details are stored by
Stripe, never by FreeSolo.

## Refunds

If a confirmed (already-charged) booking needs to be cancelled — by you, or
because an experience falls through after confirming — FreeSolo automatically
issues a refund to your original payment method through Stripe. You don't need
to open a support ticket or wait on a manual review; it's handled the moment
the cancellation goes through.

<Callout type="info">
  Refunds typically appear on your statement within 5–10 business days,
  depending on your bank — that timing is set by your card issuer, not FreeSolo.
</Callout>

## Becoming a host? You'll set up payouts too

If you register a venue, you'll go through a short **Stripe Connect**
onboarding flow to verify your identity and link a bank account — that's how
FreeSolo gets your share of each booking to you. You can find this from your
**Business dashboard**; see [Hosting an experience](/docs/hosting) for the full
walkthrough.
