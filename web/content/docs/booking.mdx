Booking on FreeSolo works a little differently from a typical "buy now"
checkout — and that's deliberate. Group experiences only make sense as a
*group*, so the whole flow is built around getting enough people in the door
before anything actually happens.

FreeSolo handles the reservation, not the money. Anything the venue charges you
settle with the venue directly, on the day.

## The reservation flow

<Steps>
<Step>
**Pick your seats.** From an experience page, tap **Reserve a seat**. Choose
how many spots you need (1 to 4), and optionally leave a short note for the
host — handy for dietary needs, accessibility questions, or just saying hi
before you meet in person.
</Step>
<Step>
**Check the details.** The experience page shows the date, time, meeting point,
group size, and — where the venue has one — the price you can expect to pay them
on the day.
</Step>
<Step>
**You're reserved.** Your seat is held and your booking shows as **pending** —
the experience isn't confirmed yet, because it's still waiting on the rest of
the group.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Why "pending" comes before "confirmed"

Every experience has a **minimum group size** (`minSeats`) — the headcount the
host needs to actually run it. Until that minimum is reached:

- Your booking sits as **pending**.
- You'll get a notification telling you how many more travelers are needed.

The moment enough people have joined to hit the minimum, **everyone's pending
bookings for that experience confirm at once** — you'll get a *"You're in! 🎒"*
notification and email.

<Callout type="success">
  If the group never fills, your reservation simply lapses. Nothing to cancel,
  nothing to reclaim.
</Callout>

## Booking statuses, plain and simple

| You'll see… | It means… |
|---|---|
| **Pending** | Your seat is held; the group is still filling up |
| **✓ Confirmed** | Enough people joined — it's happening |
| **Done** | The experience has already taken place |
| **Cancelled** | You (or the host, due to low turnout) called it off |

Your **Profile** tab keeps a running list of your trips under **Upcoming** and
**Past trips**, so you always know where things stand.

## Changing your mind

Open the booking from your profile and choose **Cancel**. You can cancel any
**pending** or **confirmed** booking — just add a quick reason so the host
knows what happened on their end.

Cancelling frees your seat for someone else. If the group has already confirmed,
cancel as early as you can so the host isn't left short on the day.

## A note on seat limits

You can reserve **1 to 4 seats** per booking, and only as many as remain
available — the seat selector automatically disables options that would
overbook the group. If an experience is full, you won't be able to reserve at
all; keep an eye on the feed in case spots open up from a cancellation.
