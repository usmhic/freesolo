Travel credits are FreeSolo's way of closing the loop: the more you share your
own city with travelers, the more affordable it becomes to go explore someone
else's.

## How you earn them

Every time one of your hosted experiences is booked and confirmed, **10% of
that booking's value is set aside as travel credit** for you — automatically,
with no extra steps. It's the same 70/10/20 split travelers see broken down
when they book; see [Payments](/docs/payments#where-your-money-goes) for the
full picture.

A couple of confirmed group bookings a month can quietly add up to a
meaningful balance over time — money that's earmarked for your *own* future
adventures on FreeSolo.

## Where to see your balance

Your **Profile** has a dedicated **Travel Credits** card right at the top —
your current balance in euros, plus a progress bar tracking you toward the next
reward tier (every 500 in credits). It's a nice way to watch your "earned by
hosting, hosted by you" balance build up trip by trip.

## Putting them to use

Travel credits are designed to offset the cost of booking experiences as a
traveler — think of them as a running discount you've earned by being part of
the host community, rather than a separate wallet you have to manage.

<Callout type="info">
  Credits are tied to your account and reflect your real hosting activity —
  there's no way to buy, transfer, or game them. They're simply FreeSolo's way
  of saying *thanks for sharing your city*.
</Callout>

## Why it works this way

Most platforms treat "host" and "guest" as two completely different roles.
FreeSolo deliberately blurs that line — the same person who shows travelers
around their neighbourhood on a Saturday might be the one booking a pottery
class in Lisbon the following month. Travel credits are the mechanic that
makes that cycle feel rewarding rather than transactional.
