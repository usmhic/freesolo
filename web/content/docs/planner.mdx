The **Planner** lays out a journey on a map: the stops in order, the route
between them, and how long each leg takes. Use it to work out whether a trip
holds together before you host it, or just to plan your own.

Open it from **Planner** in the site header. You can try it without an
account; sign in to save plans.

## Adding stops

- **Search for a place.** Type a town, landmark or address and pick it from the
  list. Results lean toward the area you're already planning in.
- **Or click the map.** Every click adds a stop where you clicked. You can
  rename it afterwards.
- **Try the sample** if you want to see how it works first. It loads a four-stop
  route through Portugal's Douro valley.

A plan holds up to 25 stops.

## Shaping the route

- **Reorder** by dragging a stop, or with its up and down arrows. The route
  redraws as soon as the order changes.
- **Pick how you're travelling**: drive, cycle, mountain bike, walk or hike.
  Distances and times follow real roads and trails for that mode.
- **Split it into days.** Add a day, then move stops into it. Each day shows its
  own distance and time, so you can spot a day that's too long.
- **Remove a stop** with the bin icon.

On a phone, switch between **Plan** and **Map** at the top of the screen. The
totals stay pinned to the bottom either way.

<Callout type="info">
  Travel times are estimates for moving time only. They don't include stops,
  traffic or the time it takes to get from a car park to a trailhead.
</Callout>

If road and trail routing isn't available, the planner still works. It draws
straight dashed lines between stops and shows straight-line distances with a
note that they're estimates. Place search may also be switched off; when it is,
add stops by clicking the map.

## Saving and coming back

Signed in, **Save** keeps the plan on your account. **Plans** lists everything
you've saved; open one to keep editing, or start a new one. Until you save,
the planner keeps your draft in this browser, so a reload won't lose it.

Saved plans are private. Nobody else can see them unless you put one on a trip
you host.

## Showing a route on a trip you host

If you host a trip, you can show its route on the trip page:

<Steps>
<Step>
**Save the plan.** The trip shows the saved version, so save any changes first.
</Step>
<Step>
**Choose the trip.** Under **Show this route on a trip you host**, pick the trip
and tap **Add**.
</Step>
<Step>
**Check the trip page.** It now has a **The route** section with the map, total
distance and time, and every stop with the leg before it. The app shows the
same route on the trip.
</Step>
</Steps>

The trip keeps a copy of the route, so later edits to your plan don't change it
until you add it again. Stop names and days are shared; nothing else from your
plan is.
