Experiences fill a morning or an evening. **Trips** fill a long weekend or a
week: a member plans the route, publishes it with a day-by-day itinerary, and
travels with a small group of other solo travelers.

What doesn't change is who's in the room. Only approved FreeSolo members can
see who's going or ask to join, and on most trips the host still reads every
request and picks the group themselves.

## What a trip page shows

- **Dates and length.** Start and end date, how many days, and where the group
  meets on day one.
- **Itinerary.** One entry per day, so you know whether it's a hiking week or a
  slow one before you ask.
- **What the price covers.** Accommodation, local transport, some meals,
  activities — whatever the host has included. FreeSolo never takes payment;
  you settle with the host directly.
- **Who's going.** First names, photos and how many countries each traveler has
  visited. You'll only see this once you're signed in as an approved member.
- **Group size.** Trips run with a minimum and a maximum, and no group goes
  above twelve.

## Asking to join

<Steps>
<Step>
**Write a short intro.** Tap **Request to join** and tell the host a little
about yourself: where you've travelled, what you want from this trip, anything
they should know. A real sentence or two is what gets people in.
</Step>
<Step>
**Wait for the host.** Your booking shows as **Awaiting host**. A request
doesn't hold a seat, so you can't be charged or held to anything yet.
</Step>
<Step>
**Get the answer.** You'll get a notification either way. Once you're
accepted you hold a place, and the trip confirms for everyone when enough
travelers are in, just like an experience.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="info">
  Trips are joined one traveler at a time. If you're travelling with a friend
  who's also a member, each of you sends your own request.
</Callout>

If the host declines, you'll see **Not this time** on your profile. It usually
just means the group was already set. You can't re-request the same trip, but
every other trip is open to you.

Some hosts switch on **instant join** instead. On those trips any approved
member can take a place straight away, without a request.

## Hosting a trip

Any approved member can host a trip. You don't need a registered venue for a
trip; you only need one for a single-session experience.

From the **Host** tab, tap **Host a trip** and fill in:

- **Name, description and category.** Say what the trip is like and who it's
  for.
- **Destination, start date, end date and meeting time.**
- **Itinerary.** Add one line per day, with optional details.
- **What the price covers**, and the **price per person**.
- **Minimum and maximum group size.** No group goes above twelve.
- **Who gets in.** *I approve each traveler* (the default for trips) or
  *Instant join*.

When someone asks to join, you'll get a notification. Open the trip from the
**Host** tab to read their intro and profile, then tap **Welcome aboard** or
**Decline**. The screen also shows the travelers who already hold a place, so
you can see how the group is shaping up.
