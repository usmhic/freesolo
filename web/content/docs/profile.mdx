Your profile is part passport, part dashboard — everything about your FreeSolo
journey lives here.

## What's on it

- **Your photo.** Tap your avatar to pick a new one from your library — it
  updates instantly across the app, including on any reviews you've left and
  experiences you host.
- **Your name, email, and member badge.** A **✓ Verified member** pill confirms
  your account has been approved; if you're newly accepted, you might briefly
  see **⏳ Pending approval** instead.
- **Travel credits.** A standout card showing your current balance and a
  progress bar toward your next reward tier — see [Travel credits](/docs/credits)
  for how that balance grows.
- **Quick stats.** Total bookings, countries visited, and your credit balance,
  at a glance.
- **Quick actions.** One-tap shortcuts to the **Explore map**,
  **Notifications**, and **Host an experience** (if you'd like to try hosting).

## Your trips

Two tabs keep your booking history organized:

- **Upcoming** — anything **pending** or **confirmed**: experiences you've
  reserved that haven't happened yet.
- **Past trips** — anything **completed** or **cancelled**: your history,
  including experiences you can now leave a [review](/docs/reviews) for.

Each trip card shows the experience's emoji, title, location and date, the
amount you paid, and a status badge so you always know exactly where things
stand — see [Booking statuses](/docs/booking#booking-statuses-plain-and-simple)
for what each one means.

If you don't have any trips yet, you'll see a friendly prompt to head to
**Explore** and find your first one.

## Pull to refresh

Pull down anywhere on your profile to refresh your stats and trip list — handy
right after a booking confirms or a payment goes through.

## Signing out

Tap **Sign out** in the top-right corner of your profile. You'll be asked to
confirm — once you do, you're returned to the welcome screen and your session
is cleared from the device.
