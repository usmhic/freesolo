Traveling solo is wonderful — right up until you want to do something *with*
someone. FreeSolo exists for that exact moment: it's a small, vetted community
where solo travelers join intimate group experiences hosted by locals, and
where locals turn their city knowledge into something travelers can actually
book.

No big-bus tours, no stranger-danger group chats — just a handful of people
sharing a pottery morning, a food crawl, or a sunrise hike, hosted by someone
who knows exactly where to go.

## The idea, in one breath

1. **You apply** to join — FreeSolo is invite-gated, so every member has been
   looked at by a real person.
2. **You explore** a feed of small-group experiences in your city (or the city
   you're visiting).
3. **You reserve a seat.** Your booking confirms once enough people join to make
   the experience happen. FreeSolo handles the reservation; anything the venue
   charges you settle with them directly, on the day.
4. **You go, you connect, you review.** And if you happen to know your own city
   well, you can flip the script and host an experience yourself.

## What makes FreeSolo different

**Small groups, on purpose.** Every experience has a `minSeats` and `maxSeats`
— say, 4 to 8 people. Small enough that you'll actually talk to everyone;
structured enough that the host can plan properly.

**You don't commit to "maybe."** Group experiences only happen once enough
people commit. FreeSolo holds your reservation and confirms it when the group
comes together — and quietly lets it lapse when it doesn't.

**Hosted by real locals, not agencies.** Anyone in the community whose
application is approved can become a host — which means the person walking you
through Alfama's jazz cellars probably grew up two streets away from the first
one.

**No platform in the middle of your money.** FreeSolo introduces travelers to
hosts and manages the reservation. Whatever an experience costs, you pay the
venue directly.
