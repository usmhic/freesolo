FreeSolo keeps notifications purposeful — every alert ties back to something
that actually changes your plans, not promotional noise.

## Where to find them

The 🔔 **Notifications** screen (reachable from your **Profile → Quick
actions**) lists everything in one place, newest first, with a clear unread
indicator. Tap **Mark all as read** to clear the slate, or tap an individual
notification to jump straight to whatever it's about — a booking, an
experience, a review prompt.

## What you'll hear about

| Notification | When it fires |
|---|---|
| **"Almost there 🎒"** | A traveler joins an experience you've reserved, but the group hasn't hit its minimum yet — tells you how many more are needed |
| **"You're in! 🎒"** | Your group reached its minimum — your booking is confirmed |
| **"How was [experience]?"** | A few days after a completed experience, gently prompting you to leave a review |
| **Hosting updates** | As a host, you'll be notified as your experiences fill toward their minimum and confirm |

Each one links back to the relevant booking or experience, so you're never
left wondering what it's about.

## Push notifications

If you allow it, FreeSolo can send these same alerts straight to your lock
screen via push notifications — handy for time-sensitive ones like *"You're
in!"*, where knowing right away matters. You can manage this from your device's
notification settings at any time; turning push off simply means you'll still
see everything in-app, just without the lock-screen ping.

<Callout type="info">
  Push notifications require a development build of the app rather than Expo
  Go — if you're testing in Expo Go, you'll still see every notification listed
  in-app, just without the native push banner.
</Callout>
